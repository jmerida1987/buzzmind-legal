Buzzmind Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what data Buzzmind collects, what it's used for, and what rights you have over it.

Data controller

Jesús Mérida Grande, reachable at jmerida1987@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Your Google account data: name, profile photo, and email address, when you sign in with Google.
  • Birth year: to verify you're at least 13 years old, a requirement to use the app.
  • Google Contacts: if you grant permission, we match your contacts' emails against already-registered users to suggest friends. We don't download or store your contact list on our servers.
  • Usage data: buzzes sent and received, streaks, friends, credits, and any messages or styles you choose to customize.
  • A push-notification identifier, so we can notify you when someone buzzes you.
  • Purchase data, if you buy anything in the app: handled entirely by Google Play Billing.
  • Diagnostic data: if the app crashes, a technical report (device model, Android version and app state at that moment) is sent along with your user identifier, so the error can be located and fixed.

What we use your data for

  • To make the app work: sending and showing you buzzes, managing your friend list, streaks, and credit balance.
  • To prevent abuse — for example, limiting how many buzzes you can send per day.
  • To process any in-app purchases you make.

Who we share your data with

We don't sell your data to anyone. We only share it with the providers that make the app work, all acting as data processors:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud: database hosting, authentication, and push notifications.
  • Cloudflare: processes buzz delivery and purchase verification on our server.
  • Google Play Billing: if you make an in-app purchase.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase: crash reports and aggregated usage statistics, to detect errors and understand how the app is used.

Legal basis for processing

We process your data to perform the contract you accept by using the app (your account, your friends and sending buzzes), on legitimate interest grounds to keep it secure and free of abuse (sending limits and crash reports), and with your consent where required (access to your contacts and notifications).

International transfers

Our providers (Google and Cloudflare) may process data on servers located outside the European Economic Area, mainly in the United States. These transfers rely on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep your data

For as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all your data at any time from Settings → Delete account, inside the app.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your data at any time. The fastest way is deleting your account from Settings inside the app, which permanently removes all your data from our servers. You can also write to us at jmerida1987@gmail.com for any other request. If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country; in Spain, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

Children's privacy

Buzzmind isn't directed at children under 13. We verify age at sign-up, and if you state you're under 13, you won't be able to create an account.

Changes to this policy

If we make a significant change to this policy, we'll notify you inside the app.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to us at jmerida1987@gmail.com.
