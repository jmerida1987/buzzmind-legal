Delete your Buzzmind account

Buzzmind is an app by Jesús Mérida Grande. You can delete your account and data at any time in one of these two ways.

From the app (immediate)

  1. Open Buzzmind.
  2. On the Home screen, tap the gear icon to open Settings.
  3. Tap “Delete account and data” and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and can't be undone. If you signed in a long time ago, the app will ask you to sign in again for security.

If you no longer have the app installed

Email jmerida1987@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with, using the subject “Delete Buzzmind account”. We will delete your account within 30 days at most and confirm it by email.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and profile (name, photo and email address).
  • Your year of birth.
  • Your friend list, friend requests and blocked users.
  • Your received buzz history and your statistics.
  • Your credits, your purchase records and your buzz customization.
  • Your device's notification identifier.

What is kept

  • Buzzes you sent may still appear in the history of the people who received them, because they are part of their account.
  • Purchase receipts are kept by Google Play under its own policies.
  • Firebase crash reports and usage statistics are deleted automatically on Google's schedule (14 months at most).
  • Reports you submitted about other users are kept only as long as needed to review them.
